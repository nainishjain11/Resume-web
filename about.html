Profile & Perspective

Building, Pitching & Testing Ideas in the Real World

First-year student at IIM Bangalore and CA aspirant. I like practical business over textbook theory.

I study in the BBA in Digital Business & Entrepreneurship cohort at IIM Bangalore while preparing for Chartered Accountancy with ICAI. To me, commerce makes sense only when you get your hands dirty. You have to build an idea, pitch it to tough crowds, and see if the numbers actually work.

In high school, I was elected Cultural Secretary at NDPS, running inter-school festivals, debate platforms, and stage events for over 2,000 students. That experience taught me how to solve bottlenecks on the fly and stay calm under pressure.

"I want to build things that work, pitch them clearly, and back every claim with real numbers."

8 Topics I Spend Time On

Core areas of business and finance that keep me up late reading.

  1. Business Strategy:

    Why some companies build lasting moats while others get commoditized within months.

  2. Finance:

    How capital actually moves through a company, from cash conversion cycles to debt structure.

  3. Markets:

    Price discovery, investor psychology, and how liquidity shifts across different asset classes.

  4. Entrepreneurship:

    Nailing unit economics early, finding the first 100 users, and building lean operations.

  5. Competitive Problem-Solving:

    Cracking business case studies under 48-hour deadlines and surviving intense jury cross-questioning.

  6. Digital Business:

    Two-sided platform networks, software margins, and how modern startups replace legacy distributors.

  7. Accounting & CA:

    Reading financial footnotes, understanding statutory tax rules, and tracking forensic audit signals.

  8. AI Tools:

    Writing Python scripts to scrape news, summarize earnings call transcripts, and automate price alerts.
