Best Presentation | ISB '25

Receiving the Best Presentation award in The Brand Playbook track at MU20 VentureVerse.

During a 3-day entrepreneurship bootcamp at the Indian School of Business (ISB Hyderabad), our team entered The Brand Playbook track organized by MU20. The challenge was simple on paper: pick a global giant and build a sustainable brand to take them down. We drew McDonald's.

Our premise was straightforward: Gen-Z cares deeply about health and hates seed oils and mystery preservatives. So we decided to just grill it.

The hardest part was scale. How do you beat McDonald's signature taste consistency without building cold-chain factories across the country? Our answer: don't freeze the meat; standardize the spices. By partnering with local farms near every outlet and supplying them with proprietary pre-measured spice blends, we guaranteed identical flavor while keeping ingredients farm-fresh. That supply-chain solve won us Best Presentation.

  • Brand Repositioning: Swapped fried fast food for fresh, zero-seed-oil flame-grilled burgers.
  • Decentralized Supply Chain: Used regional farms and centralized spice rubs instead of frozen patty warehouses.
  • High-Energy Pitch: Combined sharp unit economics with a memorable presentation hook.
